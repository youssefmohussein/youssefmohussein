About

I'm a 3rd-year Computer Science student at Misr International University, specializing in cybersecurity, network architecture, and secure systems design. I don't approach security as a set of tools — I think in trust boundaries, attack surfaces, and defense layers.

My work spans offensive and defensive security: from building enterprise-grade network labs with VLAN segmentation and STP redundancy, to conducting controlled penetration tests that evaluate authentication strength and service availability under real attack scenarios.

Beyond the technical side, I lead teams. As Head of HR at ACPC, Co-Head of Technical at MISP, and Head of Events at IEEE — I've learned that security systems and human systems have more in common than most engineers admit.

Currently pursuing AWS Cloud Architecting and building toward OSCP. Based in Cairo, open to remote and regional opportunities.
